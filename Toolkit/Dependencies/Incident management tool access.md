# What
Access to incident management and alerting tools that facilitate incident detection, escalation, and coordination. For e.g. PagerDuty, Opsgenie, VictorOps, ServiceNow, or integrated alerting in monitoring tools.
# Why
Critical for coordinating incident response, ensuring proper escalation, maintaining communication during outages, and tracking incident resolution metrics to improve system reliability.
