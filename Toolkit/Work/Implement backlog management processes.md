# What
Establish systematic processes for managing the product backlog including story writing standards, refinement cadences, prioritization methods, and estimation practices. This includes setting up tools and workflows for effective backlog management.

# Why
Systematic backlog management enables:
1. **Clear priorities** - Team understands what work is most important and why
2. **Predictable planning** - Regular processes make sprint planning more effective
3. **Stakeholder alignment** - Clear backlog communicates priorities to all stakeholders
4. **Reduced waste** - Well-defined work reduces time spent on unclear or unnecessary tasks
5. **Better estimation** - Consistent processes improve estimation accuracy over time
6. **Transparency** - Visible backlog provides transparency into planned work and progress
