# What
Develop and document comprehensive incident response procedures including incident classification, escalation paths, communication templates, roles and responsibilities, and step-by-step resolution workflows. Include regular training and simulation exercises.
# Why
Incident response procedures are essential for:
1. **Faster recovery** - Standardized procedures reduce confusion and response time
2. **Consistent response** - All team members follow proven approaches
3. **Clear communication** - Stakeholders receive appropriate and timely updates
4. **Stress reduction** - Having a plan improves decision-making under pressure
5. **Continuous improvement** - Structured approach enables better post-incident learning
6. **Confidence building** - Teams and stakeholders trust incident handling capabilities
