# What
Create explicit working agreements that define how the team will operate together including Definition of Ready and Done, code review standards, meeting guidelines, and collaboration expectations. These agreements should be documented, visible, and regularly reviewed.

# Why
Working agreements provide:
1. **Clear expectations** - Everyone understands how the team operates and what's expected
2. **Reduced friction** - Explicit agreements prevent common sources of team conflict
3. **Consistency** - Standardized approaches to common activities and decisions
4. **Accountability** - Team members can hold each other accountable to agreed standards
5. **Onboarding** - New team members quickly understand team norms and practices
6. **Continuous improvement** - Agreements can evolve as the team learns and grows
