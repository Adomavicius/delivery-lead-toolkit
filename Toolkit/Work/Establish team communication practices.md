# What
Establish regular communication practices and protocols for the team including daily standups, retrospectives, demo sessions, and asynchronous communication guidelines. This includes defining meeting cadences, formats, and expectations for team communication.

# Why
Structured communication practices provide:
1. **Team alignment** - Regular communication keeps everyone informed and aligned
2. **Issue resolution** - Early identification and resolution of blockers and problems
3. **Continuous improvement** - Regular reflection and adaptation of team processes
4. **Stakeholder engagement** - Consistent communication with stakeholders about progress
5. **Remote work support** - Clear communication protocols enable effective distributed work
6. **Team culture** - Good communication practices build trust and team cohesion
