# What
Low Mean Time to Recovery (MTTR) measures how quickly teams can recover from failures in production. This DORA metric tracks the time between when an incident is detected and when service is restored. Elite performers recover from incidents in less than one hour.
# Why
Low MTTR is critical for:
1. **Minimized user impact** - Faster recovery reduces the duration of service disruptions for users
2. **Reduced business cost** - Shorter outages mean less revenue loss and customer dissatisfaction
3. **Improved system reliability** - Teams with low MTTR build confidence in system resilience
4. **Enhanced team capability** - Regular practice with incident response improves team skills and processes
5. **Better customer trust** - Quick recovery from issues maintains customer confidence in the service
