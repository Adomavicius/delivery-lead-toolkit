# What
Continuous delivery (CD) is the practice of keeping software in a deployable state at all times and being able to deliver working software frequently with short lead times. It extends continuous integration to include automated deployment pipelines that can safely and reliably deliver changes to production.
# Why
Continuous delivery is essential because:
1. **Faster time to market** - Features and fixes reach users quickly, providing competitive advantage
2. **Reduced risk** - Frequent, small releases are easier to test and less likely to cause problems
3. **Improved quality** - Automated testing and deployment reduce human error
4. **Business agility** - Organizations can respond quickly to market changes and customer feedback
5. **Higher customer satisfaction** - Regular delivery of value keeps customers engaged
6. **Reduced stress** - Automated, reliable deployment processes eliminate deployment anxiety
