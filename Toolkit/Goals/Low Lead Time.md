# What
Low lead time measures the time from when code is committed to when it runs successfully in production. This is another key DORA metric that indicates how quickly teams can deliver value. Elite performers achieve lead times of less than one hour.
# Why
Low lead time is essential because:
1. **Faster value delivery** - Reduces time between writing code and delivering value to users
2. **Improved responsiveness** - Enables quick response to market changes, customer feedback, and critical issues
3. **Reduced work in progress** - Shorter lead times encourage smaller batch sizes and less work in flight
4. **Better developer experience** - Developers see their changes in production quickly, maintaining engagement and motivation
5. **Lower opportunity cost** - Features and fixes provide value sooner rather than sitting in development pipelines
