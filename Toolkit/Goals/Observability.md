# What
Observability is the ability to understand the internal state of a system based on its external outputs. This includes comprehensive logging, metrics collection, distributed tracing, and monitoring that provide visibility into system behavior, performance, and health in production environments.
# Why
Observability is essential for:
1. **Faster problem resolution** - Rich telemetry data helps quickly identify and diagnose issues
2. **Proactive issue detection** - Monitoring and alerting can catch problems before they impact users
3. **Performance optimization** - Detailed metrics enable data-driven performance improvements
4. **System understanding** - Observability tools help teams understand complex distributed systems
5. **Capacity planning** - Usage and performance data inform infrastructure scaling decisions
6. **Business insights** - Application metrics can provide valuable business intelligence
