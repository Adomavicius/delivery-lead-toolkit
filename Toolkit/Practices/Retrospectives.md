# What
Retrospectives are regular team meetings focused on reflecting on recent work, identifying what went well, what didn't go well, and what can be improved. The team collaboratively identifies actionable improvements and commits to implementing them in the next iteration.

# Why
Regular retrospectives enable:
1. **Continuous improvement** - Teams regularly inspect and adapt their processes and practices
2. **Team empowerment** - Team members have a voice in shaping how they work
3. **Problem solving** - Issues are addressed systematically rather than left to fester
4. **Team bonding** - Shared reflection builds trust and team cohesion
5. **Learning culture** - Encourages experimentation and learning from both successes and failures
6. **Process evolution** - Working methods evolve based on experience and changing needs
