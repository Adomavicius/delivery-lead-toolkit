# What
Post-incident reviews (also known as postmortems or after-action reviews) are structured discussions that occur after incidents to understand what happened, why it happened, what worked well, and what can be improved. They focus on learning rather than blame.
# Why
Post-incident reviews are valuable because they:
1. **Prevent recurrence** - Identifying root causes helps prevent similar incidents
2. **Improve processes** - Reviews reveal gaps in procedures, tools, or training
3. **Share knowledge** - Lessons learned are documented and shared across the team
4. **Build resilience** - Understanding failure modes helps build more robust systems
5. **Promote learning culture** - Blameless reviews encourage openness and continuous improvement
6. **Justify improvements** - Incident data supports business cases for infrastructure or process investments
