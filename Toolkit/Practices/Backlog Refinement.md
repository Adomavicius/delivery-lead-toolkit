# What
Backlog refinement (also called backlog grooming) is the ongoing process of adding detail, estimates, and order to product backlog items. This includes clarifying requirements, breaking down large items, removing outdated items, and ensuring the backlog is ready for upcoming sprints.

# Why
Regular backlog refinement enables:
1. **Sprint readiness** - Upcoming work is well-understood and estimated before sprint planning
2. **Reduced planning time** - Sprint planning is faster when stories are already refined
3. **Better prioritization** - Regular review ensures the most valuable work is prioritized
4. **Stakeholder alignment** - Ongoing refinement keeps stakeholders engaged with priorities
5. **Risk mitigation** - Early identification of complex or unclear requirements
6. **Team preparation** - Developers understand upcoming work and can prepare accordingly
