# What
Definition of Ready (DoR) and Definition of Done (DoD) are shared team agreements that establish criteria for when work can be started and when it's considered complete. DoR ensures stories are well-prepared before development begins, while DoD ensures consistent quality standards for completed work.

# Why
Clear definitions provide:
1. **Consistent quality** - DoD ensures all work meets the same quality standards
2. **Reduced waste** - DoR prevents developers from starting work that isn't ready
3. **Improved planning** - Teams can better estimate work when requirements are clear
4. **Shared understanding** - Everyone knows what "ready" and "done" mean
5. **Better flow** - Work moves smoothly through the pipeline when criteria are clear
6. **Quality gates** - Built-in checkpoints prevent defects from advancing
