# What
Daily standups (also called daily scrums) are brief, daily team meetings where each team member shares what they worked on yesterday, what they plan to work on today, and any blockers they're facing. The meeting is time-boxed to 15 minutes and focuses on coordination rather than detailed problem-solving.

# Why
Daily standups provide:
1. **Team alignment** - Everyone knows what others are working on and team progress toward goals
2. **Early blocker identification** - Issues are surfaced quickly before they become bigger problems
3. **Coordination** - Team members can identify opportunities to collaborate or avoid conflicts
4. **Accountability** - Regular check-ins encourage progress and commitment to work
5. **Communication rhythm** - Establishes regular communication cadence for the team
6. **Transparency** - Progress and challenges are visible to the entire team
