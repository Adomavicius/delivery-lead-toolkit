# What
Sprint planning is a collaborative event where the team selects work from the product backlog for the upcoming sprint and creates a plan for delivering that work. This includes capacity planning, task breakdown, and commitment to sprint goals.

# Why
Effective sprint planning provides:
1. **Clear direction** - Team understands what needs to be accomplished in the sprint
2. **Realistic commitments** - Team commits to work they can realistically complete
3. **Shared ownership** - Entire team participates in planning and commits to goals
4. **Risk identification** - Potential blockers and dependencies are identified early
5. **Stakeholder alignment** - Clear communication about what will be delivered
6. **Improved predictability** - Regular planning cycles help establish team velocity and predictability
