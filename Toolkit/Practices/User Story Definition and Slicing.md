# What
User story definition and slicing involves writing clear, testable user stories and breaking down large stories into smaller, deliverable increments. This includes following good user story formats (As a... I want... So that...), defining acceptance criteria, and ensuring stories are small enough to be completed within a sprint.

# Why
Effective story definition and slicing provides:
1. **Clear requirements** - Well-written stories reduce ambiguity and miscommunication
2. **Faster delivery** - Smaller stories move through the development pipeline more quickly
3. **Better estimation** - Smaller, well-defined stories are easier to estimate accurately
4. **Reduced risk** - Smaller increments reduce the risk of delivering the wrong thing
5. **Improved feedback** - Stakeholders can provide feedback on smaller pieces more frequently
6. **Enhanced team understanding** - Clear stories help the entire team understand what needs to be built
