# What
Asynchronous communication involves using tools and practices that allow team members to communicate and collaborate without requiring real-time interaction. This includes documented decisions, threaded discussions, shared documents, and communication protocols that respect different time zones and work schedules.

# Why
Asynchronous communication enables:
1. **Flexible work** - Team members can work across different time zones and schedules
2. **Deep work** - Reduces interruptions that break concentration and flow
3. **Inclusive participation** - Gives everyone time to think and contribute meaningfully
4. **Documentation by default** - Important discussions and decisions are automatically recorded
5. **Scalability** - Communication patterns work well as teams grow larger
6. **Thoughtful responses** - People can take time to craft better, more considered responses
