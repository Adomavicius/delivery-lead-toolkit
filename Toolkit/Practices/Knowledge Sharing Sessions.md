# What
Knowledge sharing sessions are regular meetings where team members share technical knowledge, lessons learned, new tools, or domain expertise. This includes tech talks, code reviews, pair programming sessions, show-and-tells, and brown bag lunches focused on learning and knowledge transfer.

# Why
Knowledge sharing sessions provide:
1. **Skill development** - Team members learn new techniques and technologies from each other
2. **Reduced silos** - Knowledge is distributed across the team rather than concentrated in individuals
3. **Team building** - Collaborative learning strengthens team relationships
4. **Innovation** - Exposure to new ideas sparks creativity and innovation
5. **Problem solving** - Collective knowledge helps solve complex problems faster
6. **Onboarding** - New team members learn domain knowledge and team practices more quickly
