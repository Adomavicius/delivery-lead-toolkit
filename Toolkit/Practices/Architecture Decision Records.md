# What
Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) are lightweight documents that capture important architectural decisions, including the context, decision, and consequences. They provide a historical record of why certain technical choices were made and their trade-offs.

# Why
ADRs are valuable because they:
1. **Preserve context** - Capture the reasoning behind important technical decisions
2. **Improve future decisions** - Understanding past decisions helps inform future choices
3. **Reduce repeating discussions** - Avoid rehashing previously settled architectural debates
4. **Support team transitions** - New team members understand the evolution of system architecture
5. **Enable accountability** - Clear record of who made decisions and why
6. **Facilitate reviews** - Architectural decisions can be reviewed and improved over time
