# What
Incident response procedures are documented, practiced processes that teams follow when production incidents occur. This includes incident classification, escalation paths, communication protocols, roles and responsibilities, and step-by-step resolution procedures.
# Why
Well-defined incident response procedures are essential because they:
1. **Reduce recovery time** - Standardized procedures eliminate confusion and guesswork during incidents
2. **Ensure consistent response** - All team members follow the same proven approach
3. **Improve communication** - Clear protocols keep stakeholders informed appropriately
4. **Minimize stress** - Having a plan reduces panic and improves decision-making under pressure
5. **Enable learning** - Structured approach facilitates better post-incident analysis
6. **Build confidence** - Teams and stakeholders trust that incidents will be handled professionally
