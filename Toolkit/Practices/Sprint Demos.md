# What
Sprint demos (also called demos or sprint reviews) are regular meetings where the team demonstrates completed work to stakeholders and gathers feedback. These sessions focus on showcasing working software and facilitating discussion about what was built and what should be built next.

# Why
Sprint demos provide:
1. **Stakeholder engagement** - Regular demos keep stakeholders involved and informed about progress
2. **Fast feedback** - Early feedback on working software enables course corrections
3. **Celebration of progress** - Team achievements are recognized and celebrated
4. **Transparency** - Stakeholders have visibility into what the team is actually building
5. **Requirement validation** - Demonstrates whether built features meet stakeholder needs
6. **Team motivation** - Showcasing work provides sense of accomplishment and purpose
