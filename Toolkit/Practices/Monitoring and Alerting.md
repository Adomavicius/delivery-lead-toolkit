# What
Monitoring and alerting involves continuously collecting, analyzing, and acting on system metrics, logs, and health indicators. This includes setting up dashboards, defining alert thresholds, and establishing notification systems that inform teams when systems deviate from normal behavior.
# Why
Effective monitoring and alerting is crucial for:
1. **Proactive issue detection** - Problems are identified before they significantly impact users
2. **Faster incident response** - Teams are immediately notified when issues occur
3. **System visibility** - Provides insight into system performance and behavior patterns
4. **Data-driven decisions** - Historical data informs capacity planning and optimization efforts
5. **SLA compliance** - Monitoring ensures systems meet agreed-upon service level agreements
6. **Reduced downtime** - Early detection and quick response minimize service interruptions
