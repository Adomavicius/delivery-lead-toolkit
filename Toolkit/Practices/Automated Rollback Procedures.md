# What
Automated rollback procedures are predefined, automated processes that can quickly revert a system to a previous known-good state when issues are detected. This includes automatic triggers based on health checks, performance metrics, or error rates, as well as manual rollback capabilities.
# Why
Automated rollbacks are essential for:
1. **Rapid recovery** - Systems can be restored to working state within minutes or seconds
2. **Reduced human error** - Automated processes eliminate manual mistakes during high-stress situations
3. **24/7 availability** - Systems can recover automatically even when human operators aren't immediately available
4. **Confidence in deployments** - Teams deploy more frequently knowing they can quickly recover from issues
5. **Consistent process** - Rollback procedures are standardized and tested regularly
6. **Minimized impact** - Faster rollbacks reduce the duration and scope of service disruptions
